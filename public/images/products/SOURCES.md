# Product image sources

The images in `apple/` are optimized local copies of official Apple Newsroom product photography.
They are tracked here so production licensing and future replacements stay auditable.
Files ending in `-cutout.webp` are lossless, transparent-background derivatives of the listed
Newsroom originals; the phone pixels are unchanged and only the connected black canvas was removed.
